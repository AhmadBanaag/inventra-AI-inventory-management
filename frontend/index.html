How Inventra Works

No expensive barcode guns or complex software on the warehouse floor.

1

1. Write

Print our standardized inventory grid. Walk your stockroom shelves and write product names, quantities, and categories with a standard pen.

2

2. Snap

Take a clear photo of the handwritten page with your phone camera or document scanner and upload it directly into Inventra.

3

3. Sort & Verify

Gemini AI extracts the handwritten text into structured records. Review the side-by-side verification table, make any corrections, and commit to inventory.
